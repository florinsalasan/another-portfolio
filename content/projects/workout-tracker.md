## A Workout Tracking App

### Overview

A Flutter app meant to allow users to track their weightlifting workouts and see how they progress over time. Basic tracking of weight and reps is included, with historical charts and routine templates in active development.

### The Tech Stack

After looking at the pros and cons of different cross-platform options, I settled on **Flutter** and **Dart** for the ease of multi-platform mobile development and consistent UI rendering.
